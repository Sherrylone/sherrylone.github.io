Shaofeng Zhang (张少锋)

I am a tenure-track associate professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (SAIDS) at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC). I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where I was advised by Professor Junchi Yan and Hongyuan Zha. Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned my bachelor's degree from the UESTC in 2021.

My research focuses on self-supervised representation learning, diffusion models, point cloud understanding, and vision-language-action (VLA) modeling.

I am actively recruiting highly motivated Master's and Ph.D. students, as well as outstanding undergraduates. If you are interested in collaborating, please feel free to contact me via email.
