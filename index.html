SHAOFENG
Shaofeng Zhang (张少锋)

I am a tenure-track assistant professor (预聘副教授) at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (SAIDS), University of Science and Technology of China (USTC).

I received my Ph.D. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Junchi Yan and Hongyuan Zha. Prior to that, I earned my bachelor's degree from UESTC in 2021.

Research Interests: Self-supervised representation learning, Diffusion models, Point cloud understanding, and World model.

Recruiting: I am actively recruiting highly motivated Master's and Ph.D. students, as well as outstanding undergraduates. Please email me if interested.
